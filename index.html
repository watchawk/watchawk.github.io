Mail Poll

A small private notification tool for one family. It is not a public service.

What it does

Mail Poll checks a Gmail inbox on a schedule, with the account owner's agreement, and compares incoming messages against a list of keywords and sender names. When a message matches, it sends an alert containing the sender, the subject line and the text of that message to the person who runs the tool. Attachments are not included.

What access it asks for

Read-only access to Gmail. It cannot send, delete, or change any email. Each person who signs in does so for their own account and can remove the access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who it is for

Mail Poll is used by a single household. It has no public sign-up and is not offered to other users.

Mail Poll's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
